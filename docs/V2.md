# V2: the new look (work in progress on the `v2` branch)

V2 is the new look for all four builds: the Knomi, Mac, Windows and the web. It is being built on the `v2` branch,
cut from `knomi-v2` at 1.51, and stays there until it launches. The approved designs, mockups and the screen
checklist are in the roadmap session's notes (`v2-prep/`: `README.md`, `ASSETS.md`, `mockups/`, `review/`, `scripts/`).

## The switch

Everything new sits behind one build flag, `UI_V2`: `1` (the default on this branch) draws V2, `0` draws 1.51's screens.
Nothing in the save depends on it.

- With `UI_V2=0` the game compiles to exactly 1.51's code: the preprocessed sketch is identical, and the Knomi's
  `firmware.bin` comes out the same size as 1.51's.
- V2's code is in `scene_v2.h`, with the positions in `layout_v2.h`, the colours in `palettes_v2.h` and the scenery
  in `sprites_v2.h`. The sketch includes `scene_v2.h` just before `render()`, so it can use the drawing helpers.

| Build | V2 (default) | V1 |
|---|---|---|
| Knomi | `arduino-cli compile ... --build-property "compiler.cpp.extra_flags=-DBOARD_KNOMI_V2"` | add `-DUI_V2=0` to those flags |
| Mac | `zsh mac/build_app.sh` | `UI_V2=0 zsh mac/build_app.sh` (rebuilds the host library) |
| Windows | `zsh windows/build.sh` | `UI_V2=0 zsh windows/build.sh` |
| Web | `WEB_OUT=build/web-v2 zsh webgame/build.sh` | `UI_V2=0 WEB_OUT=build/web-v1 zsh webgame/build.sh` |

`WEB_OUT` keeps a test build out of `web/play`, which is what gets published. A copy served from localhost doesn't
join the buddy relay unless the address has `?relay=1`, just as Mac and Windows test copies need `POKEMACGOCHI_RELAY=1`.
That way a test pet never turns up beside the real ones at home.

   - Berry Drop, PokeRun, PikaSurf, Volley and the battles follow the hour: graded for dusk and night under that
     hour's sky, as the home scenes are. The bursts and the dungeon don't change;
   - battles: the zone's scene (a wild or buddy battle has the meadow) with two grassy bases under the fighters,
     lit on top with a dark underside. The flat band at the bottom is gone, so the lines are in text boxes, the
     moves and RUN are V2 buttons and the HP boxes are V2 panels, all where they were;
   - the catch: the same scene without the bases, and a soft shadow under the running Pokemon;
   - PokeDungeon: the approved tiles (lit flagstones, brick wall faces, a few flickering torches, the stairs),

5. **Menus** on the dimmed bedroom.
6. **The starter screen, and the pick that replaces the egg** (see below).

## No more eggs (Fedor, 2026-11-06)

What the egg did in 1.51, and what replaces it in V2:

| 1.51 | V2 |
|---|---|
| A farewell, release or runaway ends in a ceremony, then a new egg | The old Pokémon **walks away up the hill, from behind, and disappears behind the hills** (as in the farewell mockup, `review/r11-farewell.png`) |
| After a farewell or release you pick the next egg from your Pokédex: any base form you've raised, regular or shiny. Its stars set the genes. | You pick a **basic Pokémon from your Pokédex (shiny forms too)**, then answer an **"Are you sure?"**. No egg. |
| Three taps hatch the egg, or it hatches by itself after 3 minutes | The chosen Pokémon **walks in from the distance** and goes straight into the home walk |
| First game: three starters, then an egg | The **four starters walk in** (the starter screen) |
| After a runaway: no pick, a random common egg | Not eligible to pick from the list: **the four starters walk in**. Fedor said "if the player is still not eligible"; read here as the first game and after a runaway, which are today's two cases without a pick. To confirm with Fedor. |
| Any egg can hatch shiny as a surprise: 1 in 4 after a farewell or release, otherwise 1 in 48, down to 1 in 8 with streak and bond | **No shiny odds** (Fedor): a Pokémon is shiny only when you pick a shiny form you own. That goes for the Pokédex pick, and for the starter screen when you own that starter's shiny form. Shinies keep their gold sparkles everywhere and stay shiny when they evolve (both already the case in 1.51). |
| The "RARE EGG" / "LEGENDARY EGG" label and the rare/legendary roll | Go. The roll never decided anything in 1.51: a pick replaces it, and a runaway forces common. |
| The egg's own home screen and messages, the cracks | Go (the strings stay until V1 is removed) |

- **Saves:** the format doesn't change. A save that's in the middle of an egg when V2 arrives just hatches.
- **Sprites:** the walk away needs PMD **Walk row 4** (facing away) as well as row 0 (facing you) for the walk in and
  the home walk.

## Notes for the next steps

- `MANSION_PAL` in `sprites_v2.h` lists its colours for A-H, K-N and P. There is no I, J or O, so `letter - 'A'`
  is not its index: map the letters explicitly.
- The bars, band and labels are worked out every frame. Before V2 ships, time a frame on a real Knomi. If it's too
  slow, cache the masks, which never change.
- The bars' empty track stays the dark tint at night, as in the night mockups. Only the band under the icons goes
  white (10% and 12%) over dark scenes: night, asleep, the Cave and the Mansion (`v2DarkScene()`).
- A scene is drawn with double-precision colour mixing (to match the mockups). On a Knomi, time a redraw (zone
  or time-of-day change) and the per-frame copy of the 434 KB buffer.
- The pet still stands on 1.51's ground line (`PET_GROUND` 288), 8 px above the mockups' 296, so nothing else on
  the home screen had to move.
- One buffer holds whichever backdrop is on screen: the home's scene or a game's (`v2Cached`, by key). Leaving a
  game paints the home scene once more. PikaSurf, PokeRun's pines and track, and the dungeon's tiles move, so they
  are drawn every frame; on a Knomi, time PikaSurf's frame and the first frame of a battle and of Who's That?.
- The battle's foe stands 24 px higher than in `v2b-battle.png` (the game's `BTL_FOE_GROUND`), so its base sits on
  the far hills rather than in front of them.
- Not in step 3 (still to do): the in-game boards and panels of the review set (#27 Who's That?, #28 Volley), the
