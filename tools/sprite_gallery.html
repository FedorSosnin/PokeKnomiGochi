★ KnomiDex

All 151 of PokeKnomiGochi's Pokémon, regular and shiny, moving the way they do in the game. Tap one for its page, its moves and its cry; tap the ball when you have it; pick a few for their match-ups.

What it says about them

Each Pokémon's page says what its page in the game's Pokédex says, and the rest comes from the game too: its base stats, when it evolves, the moves it learns, how hard they hit, and the type chart its battles use. So the match-ups here are the game's. Its battles are simpler than Red and Blue's: each Pokémon has one type, and each move has one number for its power, with Red and Blue's accuracy, hits in a row and turns spent charging folded in.

The balls you tap are only in this browser, on this device: nothing is sent anywhere.

Where they come from

The sprites are the work of the PMD Sprite Collaboration's artists, who share them under CC BY-NC 4.0. Each Pokémon's own page (tap it) names the artists who made its regular and shiny sprites. Thank you, all of you.

What PokeKnomiGochi changed

  • Colours are rounded to the Knomi screen's 16-bit colour, and a pixel that was partly see-through is now either solid or clear.
  • They play 1.4 times slower than the originals, which felt rushed on a pet, with no frame shorter than 70 ms and at most 24 frames a view.
  • Only some of each sheet is kept: the animations facing you, the walk in its eight directions, and standing, attacking and hurt from behind, taken from the sheet's view from the back and right.
  • Each frame stands on its own feet instead of where it sits in its frame. A few are placed by hand: Ivysaur and Venusaur stand on their legs when they face you, Diglett and Dugtrio sit lower in their mounds, and Blastoise and Magikarp lie lower when asleep.
  • They're the versions packed for the game. SpriteCollab keeps improving its sprites, so some there may be newer.

What it added

  • Turn: the Pokédex entry's turn, made from the first frame of the walk in each of its eight directions, 250 ms each.
  • Battle: the attack seen from behind, the way your own Pokémon fights.
  • A shiny's four twinkling gold sparkles, and the shadow under each Pokémon. Neither is part of the sprites.
